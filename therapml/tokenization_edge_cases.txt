BPE Edge Cases & Special Tokens

1. Special Tokens (<PAD>, <BOS>, <EOS>, <UNK>):
   - Atomic & Isolated: Isolated via regex; never learned as merges or split into pieces.
   - Vocab End: Occupy the last IDs of the Vocab.
   - <UNK> Never Needed: Base 256 bytes cover all inputs, so fallback to <UNK> is unnecessary.

2. Unicode & Mixed-Language:
   - Evaluated as raw UTF-8 bytes; lossless roundtrip across all alphabets and scripts.

3. Emojis & Complex Modifiers:
   - ZWJ, skin tones, and flag sequences are preserved byte-for-byte.

4. Whitespace:
   - Never normalized or collapsed; spaces, tabs, and newlines retain exact byte identity.

5. Invalid / Partial UTF-8:
   - Decoded with `errors="replace"`, safely producing `\ufffd` without raising exceptions.
